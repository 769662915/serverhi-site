# Research: AI Generated Patches Fail Study 2026

## Sources

1. **ZDNet** (https://www.zdnet.com/article/ai-isnt-ready-to-patch-your-software-74-of-them-miss-the-mark)
   - 1Password's Off-By-1 Labs published study
   - Only 26% of AI-generated patches were actually usable
   - 74% of patches failed to properly fix vulnerabilities
   - Hypothesis was ~67% success rate, actual results "significantly lower and more uneven"
   - LLMs tasked with developing patches for new, complex vulnerabilities

2. **Dark Reading** (https://www.darkreading.com/application-security/ai-generated-patches-fail-half-time)
   - Study of more than 6,000 patches
   - Even working patches can introduce new bugs, break something else, or are open to bypass
   - AI models are increasingly used to find vulnerabilities and generate patches

3. **Help Net Security** (https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/08/06/1password-ai-generated-vulnerability-patches)
   - Three in four AI-generated vulnerability patches leave something broken

## Key Facts
- 1Password's Off-By-1 Labs conducted the study
- 6,000+ patches analyzed
- Only 26% of AI-generated patches were usable
- 74% failure rate
- Even "working" patches often introduce new bugs or bypasses
- AI can discover bugs at scale but struggles to fix them reliably
- Researchers hypothesized 67% success rate, actual was much lower
